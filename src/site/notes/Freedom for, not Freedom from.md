You always need a reason to do something big.
And often big reasons tend to involve freedom.
I argue, as others have also done, that you should choose to get freedom _for_ something, instead of freedom _from_ something.

If you fight against some existing system, don't fight for freedom _from_ that system, but fight for the freedom _for_ your new system.
This re-contextualizes from destroying to creating and won't leave you empty-handed once the old system is destroyed.
